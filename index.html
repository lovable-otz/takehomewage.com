Take Home Wage shows what minimum wage work actually pays in the UK once deductions come off. From 1 April 2026 the National Minimum Wage is £12.71 an hour if you are 21 or over, £10.85 at 18 to 20, and £8.00 if you are under 18 or on the apprentice rate. Enter your hours and see the net figure.

Why trust these numbers

Every rate on this site is taken from GOV.UK and nowhere else. Hourly minimum wage rates come from the National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage rates page. Income Tax rates, bands and the Personal Allowance come from the Income Tax rates and Personal Allowances page. Employee National Insurance rates and thresholds come from the National Insurance rates and categories page. Auto-enrolment contribution rules and the qualifying earnings band come from the workplace pensions pages.

All of those figures were last checked against the source pages on 19 September 2026, and they are re-checked when the rates change: minimum wage on 1 April, tax and National Insurance on 6 April. The site is maintained by the Take Home Wage editorial team, which writes and checks the calculators. Nothing here is modelled, forecast or scraped from other calculators. If a figure looks wrong, email us and we will check it against the source and correct it.
