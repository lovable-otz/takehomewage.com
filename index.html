Why trust these numbers

Every rate on this site is taken from GOV.UK and nowhere else. Hourly minimum wage rates come from the National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage rates page. Income Tax rates, bands and the Personal Allowance come from the Income Tax rates and Personal Allowances page. Employee National Insurance rates and thresholds come from the National Insurance rates and categories page. Auto-enrolment contribution rules and the qualifying earnings band come from the workplace pensions pages.

All of those figures were last checked against the source pages on 19 September 2026, and they are re-checked when the rates change: minimum wage on 1 April, tax and National Insurance on 6 April. The site is maintained by the Take Home Wage editorial team, which writes and checks the calculators. Nothing here is modelled, forecast or scraped from other calculators. If a figure looks wrong, email us and we will check it against the source and correct it.

Common questions

What will the minimum wage be in 2026 in the UK per hour?

From 1 April 2026 the UK minimum wage is £12.71 an hour for workers aged 21 and over. The 18 to 20 rate is £10.85, and the rate for under-18s and apprentices is £8.00. More in UK Minimum Wage 2025.

Is the National Minimum Wage going up in 2025?

Yes. The rates rose on 1 April 2025: the 21 and over rate went to £12.21, the 18 to 20 rate to £10.00, and the under-18 and apprentice rates to £7.55. Those were the rates for the whole year to 31 March 2026. More in UK Minimum Wage 2025.

Will the minimum wage go up again in April 2026?

Yes, they did. New rates took effect on 1 April 2026: £12.71 for workers aged 21 and over, £10.85 at 18 to 20, and £8.00 for under-18s and apprentices. That was a 50p rise on the adult rate and 85p on the 18 to 20 rate. More in UK Minimum Wage 2025.

How much is 40 hours a week minimum wage?

At the current £12.71 rate, 40 hours a week is £508.40 gross, or £26,436.80 a year over 52 weeks. More in UK Minimum Wage 2025.

Will the minimum wage go up again in 2026?

Yes. The rates rose on 1 April 2026, from £12.21 to £12.71 for workers aged 21 and over, from £10.00 to £10.85 at 18 to 20, and from £7.55 to £8.00 for under-18s and apprentices. More in UK Minimum Wage 2025.

Is 12.60 an hour good?

£12.60 an hour is below the current legal minimum for workers aged 21 and over, which is £12.71 from 1 April 2026. It was above the £12.21 rate that applied through to 31 March 2026. More in UK Minimum Wage 2025.
